The Silent Eyepiece

The instrument is powered, aligned and reporting nothing at all. That is not a fault. That is the design.

Optic Assembly / Idle

Nothing To Report

✦ · ✧ · ✦

The eyepiece renders a perfectly ordinary field of stars and nothing else. Whatever the observatory actually recorded was never printed on the glass. It was attached to the instrument housing itself, where the page only has to be read, not rendered.

Observatory annotation The eyepiece is a device, and devices are described in markup. Open Developer Tools, go to Elements, and find the element with the identifier observation. Read its attributes.
